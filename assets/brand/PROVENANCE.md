# Brand Image Provenance

All brand images in this directory were made for the project by Burkan Alp
Kale (ArgonGate Ltd.) in Photopea. Some compositions incorporate historical
paintings, engravings, and photographs of classical sculpture as heavily
transformed references; every incorporated element was substantially reworked
(recolored, recomposed, redrawn to a two-tone or high-contrast engraving
treatment, and combined with original elements) rather than reproduced.
