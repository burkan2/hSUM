# Atlas

The image paths carry a second reading. They are design codenames, not product
terminology, and they do not claim that their figures belong to one historical
pantheon or philosophical system.

| Path | Display form | Product reading | Historical reading |
|---|---|---|---|
| `katabasis/aidoneus.png` | Aïdōneus | Local custody and descent beneath the session | *Katabasis* is descent; Aïdōneus is an attested poetic name for Hades |
| `ouranos/asteropoios.png` | Asteropoios | Mapping and measuring retrieval space | *Ouranos* is the sky; Asteropoios is a project coinage, “star-maker,” not an ancient alias of Urania |
| `mneme/aletheia.png` | Alētheia | Provenance, verification, and the source revealed | *Mnēmē* is memory; Alētheia is Truth, a distinct personification rather than another name for Mnemosyne |
| `horos/achamoth.png` | Achamoth | Evidence crossing the engine boundary and returning to the caller | In Irenaeus’s account of Valentinian tradition, Horos obstructs the separated Achamoth, whose passion includes a desire to return |

The distinction matters:

- **Aïdōneus** is a true alternative name.
- **Alētheia** is an adjacent concept.
- **Achamoth** is a tradition-specific lower or separated Sophia.
- **Asteropoios** is intentionally constructed for this project.

The similar attested name **Asteropaios** belongs to a Paeonian warrior in
Homer's *Iliad*, not to Urania. Do not silently substitute that spelling.
